Contoso Travel & Expenses Policy
================================

This policy describes the maximum reimbursable amounts for business travel
expenses incurred by employees of Contoso Ltd. All claims must be submitted
within 30 days of the expense being incurred and accompanied by an itemized
receipt.

1. Ground transportation
   - Taxi: up to $75 per ride, or $150 per day in total.
   - Ride-share (Uber/Lyft): up to $60 per ride.
   - Rental car: economy or compact class only, up to $80 per day.
   - Public transit: actual cost, no upper limit.

2. Lodging
   - Standard cities: up to $200 per night.
   - High-cost cities (NYC, SF, London, Tokyo): up to $325 per night.

3. Meals (per diem)
   - Breakfast: $20
   - Lunch: $30
   - Dinner: $60
   - Alcohol is not reimbursable.

4. Air travel
   - Economy class for flights under 6 hours.
   - Premium economy permitted for flights over 6 hours.
   - Business class only with VP-level approval.

5. Mileage (personal vehicle)
   - $0.67 per mile, parking and tolls reimbursable at cost.

Questions about this policy should be directed to expenses@contoso.example.com.
