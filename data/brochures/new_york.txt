Margie's Travel - New York City Long Weekend
============================================

Margie's Travel's classic New York City package, perfect for a Thursday-to-
Sunday escape:

- Round-trip economy flights into JFK or LaGuardia.
- Three nights at the 4-star Margie's Midtown Hotel, two blocks from Times Square.
- A hop-on/hop-off bus tour covering Manhattan and Brooklyn.
- Skip-the-line tickets to the Empire State Building observation deck.
- A guided walking tour of Central Park, including Strawberry Fields and Bethesda Terrace.
- A ferry trip past the Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island.
- A reserved table for dinner at a classic Manhattan steakhouse.

Optional add-ons:
- Broadway show tickets (from $120 per person).
- Top of the Rock observation deck ($45 per person).
- A guided food tour of Greenwich Village ($90 per person).

Best time to visit: April through June, or September through early November.
Currency: US Dollar (USD).
