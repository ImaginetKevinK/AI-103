Margie's Travel - Tokyo Discovery
=================================

Experience the vibrant blend of tradition and technology with Margie's Travel
Tokyo Discovery package:

- Round-trip economy flights to Narita or Haneda International Airport.
- Five nights at the 4-star Margie's Shinjuku Hotel.
- A 7-day unlimited Japan Rail Pass for travel across the country.
- A guided tour of Asakusa, including Senso-ji Temple and Nakamise shopping street.
- A day trip to Mount Fuji and Hakone with a soak in a traditional onsen.
- A reserved seat on a bullet train (Shinkansen) round-trip to Kyoto.
- A traditional tea ceremony experience in Ueno.

Optional add-ons:
- Tsukiji outer market food tour ($85 per person).
- TeamLab Planets digital art museum tickets ($45 per person).
- Robot Restaurant cabaret show ($110 per person).

Best time to visit: late March to early April for cherry blossoms, or
October to November for autumn foliage.
Currency: Japanese Yen (JPY).
