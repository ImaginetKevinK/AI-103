Margie's Travel - London Getaway
================================

Margie's Travel is delighted to offer guided weekend breaks to London,
England. Our London package includes:

- Round-trip economy flights from your nearest international airport.
- Three nights at the 4-star Margie's Mayfair Hotel, breakfast included.
- A guided walking tour of Westminster, including Big Ben, Westminster Abbey,
  and the Houses of Parliament.
- Tickets to the Tower of London and the Crown Jewels exhibit.
- A Thames river cruise from Westminster Pier to Greenwich.
- 24/7 concierge support from a Margie's Travel local representative.

Optional add-ons:
- West End theatre tickets (from $90 per person).
- Day trip to Windsor Castle ($120 per person).
- Afternoon tea at The Ritz ($95 per person).

Best time to visit: April through September for the most reliable weather.
Currency: British Pound Sterling (GBP).
